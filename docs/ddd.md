## How to Guarantee the Best AI Headshot Results

While the AI does the heavy lifting, the secret to getting a flawless image is providing the right input. If you want truly [professional headshots](https://pfpmaker.ai/professional-headshots) that no one will ever suspect were made by a computer, follow these three rules:

1.  **Use Good Lighting:** When taking your input selfies, stand facing a window. Natural, even daylight prevents the AI from getting confused by harsh shadows on your face.
2.  **Keep a Neutral to Warm Expression:** A slight, confident smile works best. Avoid overly dramatic facial expressions, as they can distort the AI's mapping of your facial features.
3.  **Provide Multiple Angles:** Upload 5 to 10 photos featuring your face straight-on, turned slightly to the left, and slightly to the right. This gives the AI a complete 3D understanding of your jawline, nose, and cheekbones.
